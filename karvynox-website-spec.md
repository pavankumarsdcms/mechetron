## 1. GLOBAL ELEMENTS (Header, Navigation, Footer)

### Header / Navigation

**Goal:** Let visitors find any page in 1 click; keep the primary CTA visible at all times.

**Structure:**
- Logo (left) — links to homepage
- Nav links (center or right):
  - Services (dropdown: Performance Marketing, Social Media, Branding, Email/WhatsApp, Web Design)
  - Work (Case Studies)
  - Insights
  - About
  - Contact
- Primary CTA button (right, always visible): **"Book a Strategy Call"**
- Mobile: hamburger menu with same links + CTA pinned at bottom of screen

**Sticky behavior:** Header sticks on scroll with slight background blur/darkening.

---

### Footer

**Goal:** Catch visitors who scroll to the bottom without converting; provide trust signals and quick navigation.

**Sections:**
1. **Brand block** — Logo + 1-line tagline: "Performance-first marketing for brands that want to grow profitably."
2. **Quick links** — Services, Work, Insights, About, Contact
3. **Contact info** — Email, phone, city (if relevant)
4. **Social icons** — LinkedIn, Instagram, X/Twitter
5. **Trust strip** — "Google Partner | Meta Business Partner | 50+ brands scaled"
6. **Legal links** — Privacy Policy, Terms of Service
7. **CTA banner (above footer or inside):** "Ready to lower your CAC? Let's talk." + Button: Book a Strategy Call

### Layout & UX Notes (Homepage)
- Keep above-the-fold laser-focused: headline + subhead + CTA + trust logos. No clutter.
- Use scroll-triggered animations sparingly (counters, card entrances) to add polish without hurting speed.
- Page load under 3 seconds (optimize images, lazy-load below fold).
- Mobile-first: every section should work in single-column.
